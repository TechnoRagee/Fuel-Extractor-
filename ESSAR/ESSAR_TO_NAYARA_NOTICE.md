# ⛽ Essar Oil to Nayara Energy — Historical Context & Retail Network Notice

## 📌 Executive Summary
**Essar Oil Limited (EOL)**, which pioneered private fuel retailing in India and built a network of over 4,500+ petrol pumps, **was officially acquired and rebranded as Nayara Energy Limited in 2018**.

Today, **all former Essar petrol pumps in India operate under the Nayara Energy brand**, and Essar Group (`https://www.essar.com/`) no longer operates any retail petrol pumps under the "Essar" name in India.

---

## 🏛️ Chronology of the Acquisition & Rebranding

1. **August 2017 ($12.9 Billion Landmark Deal)**:
   - Essar Global Fund Limited sold 100% of **Essar Oil Limited** (including the 20 MMTPA Vadinar Refinery in Gujarat and its nationwide retail network of ~4,500+ fuel stations) to an international consortium:
     - **Rosneft** (through PJSC Rosneft Oil Company): 49.13%
     - **Trafigura & UCP Investment Group** (through Kesani Enterprises): 49.13%
   - This was the largest single Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) into India's energy sector.

2. **May 2018 (Name Change to Nayara Energy)**:
   - Essar Oil Limited formally adopted the name **Nayara Energy Limited** (derived from the Hindi word *Naya* meaning 'New' and *Era*).

3. **2018 – Present (Complete Station Rebranding)**:
   - Every single retail outlet and petrol pump across India previously branded as "Essar" was systematically updated and rebranded to the **Nayara Energy** livery (emerald green and orange).
   - Nayara Energy has expanded the network from ~4,500 stations to over **6,500+ operational fuel retail stations** nationwide today.

---

## 🏢 What Does Essar Group (`essar.com`) Do Today?
- **Essar Group** operates in Energy Transition through **EET Fuels** (Essar Energy Transition).
- Their downstream retail stations are now located in the **United Kingdom (UK)**, supplied by the Stanlow Manufacturing Complex in Cheshire.
- In India, Essar's energy presence is concentrated in CBM (Coal Bed Methane / Essar Exploration & Production) and green hydrogen / clean energy projects, but **not downstream retail fuel distribution**.
